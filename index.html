WHY IT MATTERS

Blood donation is a simple act with a powerful purpose.

Hospitals need a safe and reliable blood supply for many kinds of care. Voluntary donors help make that possible.

✚

Supports essential care

Donated blood and its components may be needed during surgeries, serious injuries, childbirth complications and treatment for certain illnesses.

◉

One donation, different components

Blood can be separated into red cells, platelets and plasma. Each component has different medical uses.

♧

Safety comes first

Donors are screened by trained staff, and blood is tested and handled under regulated procedures before it can be used.

KNOW BEFORE YOU GO

Blood donation: a general guide

Use this educational checklist to prepare questions for an authorized blood bank. It cannot determine medical eligibility.

Check the project demo criteria

Enter your details to see whether they match the four demonstration rules provided for this project.

These are illustrative project thresholds only, not universal donation rules. Requirements vary by blood centre and donor circumstances. This checker is not medical clearance or a substitute for professional screening.

Illustrative thresholds used in this prototype

  • Age: 18 to 65 years, inclusive.
  • Weight: strictly more than 45 kg.
  • Hemoglobin: strictly more than 12.5 g/dL.
  • At least 90 days since the previous donation.

Medical screening still matters

Health history, medicines, recent illness and other factors may affect eligibility. Only qualified staff at an authorized blood centre can make the final decision.

HELP CENTRE

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about this student project and its demonstration features.

Is One More Heartbeat a real blood bank?

No. It is a frontend college-project prototype. It does not collect, store or distribute blood.

Are camps and inventory figures real?

No. They are fictional sample records used to demonstrate the interface.

Does donor registration confirm an appointment?

No. It saves a local demo appointment and reduces the sample slot count in this browser only. Contact an authorized camp organizer for real arrangements.

Can the eligibility checklist approve me?

No. Only qualified medical staff can assess donor eligibility.

Where is my form data stored?

In LocalStorage in the current browser. It is not sent to a server.

Can another user see my records?

Not through this prototype. LocalStorage is local to the browser profile and website origin.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Technology with a community purpose

One More Heartbeat is a student concept designed to demonstrate how a digital platform could support awareness and coordination.

THE PROBLEM

Information can be difficult to coordinate.

Community drives depend on awareness, volunteers, clear event details and communication between organizers and potential donors.

THE PROPOSED IDEA

One connected information hub.

One More Heartbeat brings a camp directory, donor interest form, volunteer hub, educational resources and dashboard into one responsive website.
